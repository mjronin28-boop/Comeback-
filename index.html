The target: big, lean, athletic

The VXS-style aesthetic look. It comes from training these in this order:

  • Shoulders, side delts. Width. The biggest single thing for looking big in a T-shirt.
  • Lats. Wide back, the V-taper.
  • Upper chest. Full chest that shows in a shirt.
  • Arms. Triceps are two thirds of the arm.
  • Abs. Trained, then revealed by getting lean in the Define phase.
  • Legs. Athletic, so you're not top-heavy. Football fixes the rest.

Same idea as his, built your way: lateral raises almost every upper day, pull-ups every week, abs 3 times a week, sprints for the athletic part.

Face icing, the right way

  • Wrap 3 to 4 ice cubes in a thin cloth. Never bare ice on skin.
  • Slow circles: forehead, under eyes, cheeks, jaw. 1 to 2 minutes total.
  • Or: bowl of ice water, dip face 10 seconds, 3 times.
  • Best in the morning. Cuts puffiness, makes skin look tighter.

Cold shower / ice bath

  • Cold shower, 2 to 3 min: great for waking up and mood.
  • Don't do an ice bath right after strength training. It can blunt muscle growth. Keep 4+ hours gap, or do it on rest and sport days.
  • Start with 30 seconds cold at the end of a normal shower and build up.

For the bike tan

  • SPF 50 before every ride. Reapply if out for 3+ hours.
  • Full-sleeve riding gloves and a buff for the neck.
  • Gentle scrub only 2 times a week. More makes it worse.
  • Tan fades in 4 to 8 weeks if you stop adding to it.

Look sharp

  • Haircut every 3 to 4 weeks. Beard line clean.
  • Lip balm daily. Nails short.
  • Sleep 7.5 hrs and water: the cheapest skincare there is.
